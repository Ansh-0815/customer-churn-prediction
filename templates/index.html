Exploratory Data Analysis & Model Exploration Study

Detailed study of customer churn factors, class imbalance treatment, and model selection on the IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset (7,043 customers, 21 features).

Contract Stickiness

Month-to-month contracts represent the highest risk factor. Over 42.7% of month-to-month customers churn, compared to just 11.2% of 1-year and 2.8% of 2-year contract holders.

Early Life Cycle Risk

Customer tenure is strongly negatively correlated with churn. The median tenure for churned customers is only 10 months, whereas retained customers have a median tenure of 38 months.

Service Sensitivity

Fiber optic subscribers churn at a rate of 41.8%, compared to DSL subscribers at 18.9%. Customers without security add-ons like OnlineSecurity and TechSupport are twice as likely to leave.

Methodology & Class Imbalance Treatment

Data Leakage Prevention: To ensure high-quality production reliability, the dataset was split into train/test before any scaling or imputation occurred, preventing leakage of testing set distributions into the training scaler.

SMOTE Oversampling: Since the minority class (churners) represents only 26.5% of the data, models struggled to identify positive cases. Oversampling via SMOTE generated synthetic samples to balance the target feature, successfully boosting Gradient Boosting model test recall while preserving high ROC AUC.
